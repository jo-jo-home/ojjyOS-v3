# Decision record template

Append to `roadmap/03-target-architecture.md` §2 with the next free
number. A decision that replaces an older one says so, and the old one
gets "Superseded by D-0NN" added to its title.

```markdown
### D-0NN Short title
**Context.** What problem or choice came up, with facts (measurements,
platform limits, licence questions).
**Options.** A: … B: … C: … with pros/cons.
**Decision.** What we chose.
**Why.** The deciding reasons.
**Consequences.** What changes in the roadmap (cards added/changed), what
becomes harder, what to revisit and when.
**Date / by.** YYYY-MM-DD, session/commit.
```
