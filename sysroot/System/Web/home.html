Tips

  • Type words into the address bar to search, or a web address to open it.
  • Cmd+L address bar, Cmd+R reload, Cmd+[ / Cmd+] back and forward.
  • https:// sites are encrypted with TLS 1.3 and must prove who they are: the lock in the address bar means the certificate checked out. Revoked certificates are not detected yet.
  • No network? Turn on Wi-Fi in Control Center.
